THE FORCE BEHIND LUXEMBOURG SPORTING EXCELLENCE

Established in 2006, OPTIN has shaped the landscape of sports marketing, turnkey event production, and electronic timing across Luxembourg and the Greater Region.

100% INDEPENDENT LUXEMBOURG AGENCY

Engineered for High-Pressure Mass Participation

For nearly two decades, OPTIN has stood as the trusted partner of national sporting federations, Olympic committees, municipal authorities, and corporate leaders. We transform complex logistical challenges into exhilarating public experiences.

From our headquarters in Hamm, Luxembourg, we operate an in-house mobile 4K broadcast fleet, high-frequency active/passive RFID timing stations, and dedicated web engineering teams.
